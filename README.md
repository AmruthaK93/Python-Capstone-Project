# Python-Capstone-Project
# **Employee Data Analysis Project**

## **Overview**
This project involves analyzing employee data from ABC Company, consisting of **458 records and 9 columns**. The goal is to perform **data preprocessing, statistical analysis, visualization, and insights reporting** to understand key workforce trends. The analysis includes **team distribution, position segregation, age demographics, salary expenditure, and correlation analysis.**

---

## **1. Data Preprocessing**
### **Steps Taken:**
- **Corrected Height Data**: Replaced inconsistent values in the "Height" column with random numbers between **150 and 180 cm**.
- **Ensured Data Consistency**: Checked for missing or erroneous values and confirmed dataset integrity before analysis.
- **Converted Data Types**: Ensured numerical and categorical values were in proper formats for analysis.

---

## **2. Analysis Tasks & Visualizations**
### **2.1 Distribution of Employees Across Teams**
- Identified the **New Orleans Pelicans** as the team with the most employees (**19 employees, 4.15% of total**).
- Most teams have around **15 employees (~3.28%)**, showing a balanced workforce allocation.
- **Visualization:** **Bar chart** and **pie chart** for team-wise employee distribution.

### **2.2 Employee Segregation by Position**
- **Shooting Guards (SG)** are the most common position (**102 employees**).
- **Centers (C)** are the least common (**79 employees**), suggesting a strategic preference for guards and forwards.
- **Visualization:** **horizontal bar chart** for position distribution.

### **2.3 Predominant Age Group**
- The majority of employees are in the **25-30 age group (213 employees, 46.5%)**.
- The **18-24 age group (154 employees, 33.6%)** follows, indicating a **predominantly young workforce**.
- Few employees are over **35 years old**, with none in the **41-45 range**.
- **Visualization:** **Bar chart** for age group distribution.

### **2.4 Salary Expenditure by Team & Position**
- **Cleveland Cavaliers** have the **highest total salary expenditure ($106,988,689)**.
- The **Centers (C) position accounts for the highest salary expenditure ($466,377,332)**.
- **Visualization:** **Two bar charts** for salary expenditure by team and position.

### **2.5 Correlation Between Age and Salary**
- **Weak positive correlation (r = 0.21)** found between age and salary.
- Older employees tend to earn slightly more, but **performance and contracts likely influence salaries more than age**.
- **Visualization:** **Scatter plot with regression line**.

---

## **3. Insights Gained**
   **Teams are evenly staffed**, with no extreme workforce imbalances.
   **Guards and forwards are preferred** over centers, aligning with modern basketball strategies.
   **Young workforce dominates**, suggesting a focus on fresh talent over experience.
   **Centers receive the highest salaries**, likely due to their crucial role in gameplay.
   **Age doesn’t strongly determine salary**, indicating that **performance and contracts** play a bigger role.

---

## **4. Conclusion**
This project provides valuable insights into workforce distribution, salary patterns, and strategic hiring decisions. The findings can help ABC Company optimize **team composition, salary distribution, and recruitment strategies** based on data-driven insights.
